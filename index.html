§ 02 — Bio

關於我About Physician ・ Scientist ・ Educator

李卓豪醫師現任三軍總醫院血液腫瘤科主治醫師，並於國防醫學院醫學系任助理教授。畢業於國防醫學院醫學系後，先後完成內科住院醫師訓練（2015–2018）與血液腫瘤次專科訓練（2020–2023），由戴明燊教授與陳宇欽教授指導。

臨床工作之外，致力於以實證醫學方法回答尚未解決的臨床問題，已發表逾 45 篇 SCI 期刊論文，研究涵蓋系統性回顧與網絡薈萃分析（含 Cochrane Review）、真實世界資料（TriNetX），以及免疫檢查點抑制劑相關的安全性議題。

「最好的臨床問題，往往在病房裡誕生；好的研究方法，能讓它們找到答案。」

近期重要發表包括兩篇刊登於 JAMA Dermatology 的免疫治療相關不良反應研究，以及刊載於 The Breast 上關於 CDK4/6 抑制劑（abemaciclib vs palbociclib）的真實世界比較性研究。並獲選為 ASH 亞太臨床試驗訓練計劃（CTTI 2025）與 ASCO 國際臨床研究課程（ICRC Poland 2025）學員，2026 年入圍 ASCO 亞太區國際臨床研究學者（ICRS Finalist）。

Dr. Cho-Hao Howard Lee is a Clinical Physician in the Division of Hematology and Oncology at Tri-Service General Hospital, and an Assistant Professor at the School of Medicine, National Defense Medical Center. After receiving his MD from NDMC, he completed Internal Medicine residency (2015–2018) and Hematology & Oncology fellowship (2020–2023) at TSGH, mentored by Dr. Ming-Shen Dai and Dr. Yeu-Chin Chen.

Beyond clinical practice, he is committed to answering unresolved bedside questions through evidence-based methods, with over 45 SCI-indexed publications spanning systematic reviews and network meta-analyses (including Cochrane Reviews), real-world evidence (TriNetX), and the safety of immune checkpoint inhibitors.

"The best clinical questions are born at the bedside; the right methods help them find their answers."

Recent work includes two papers in JAMA Dermatology on immunotherapy-related cutaneous adverse events, and a real-world comparative study of CDK4/6 inhibitors (abemaciclib vs. palbociclib) in The Breast. He was selected for the ASH Clinical Trial Training Institute Asia Pacific (CTTI 2025) and the ASCO International Clinical Research Course (ICRC Poland 2025), and is a 2026 Finalist of the ASCO International Clinical Research Scholar program (Asia Pacific).

§ 03 — Research

研究領域Research Focus Where bedside meets evidence.

i.

免疫檢查點抑制劑安全性 Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Safety

關注免疫治療相關不良反應（irAE）的發生率、預測生物指標，與長期預後。近期作品包括兩篇刊載於 JAMA Dermatology 的免疫治療皮膚毒性研究。

Investigating the incidence, predictive biomarkers, and long-term outcomes of immune-related adverse events (irAEs). Recent work includes two papers in JAMA Dermatology on cutaneous toxicity.

ICIirAEJAMA Derm
ii.

真實世界資料分析 Real-World Evidence in Oncology

運用 TriNetX 跨國研究網絡，採用 target trial emulation 與傾向分數配對方法，比較治療效果與評估罕見事件。代表作為 The Breast 上 abemaciclib vs palbociclib 的真實世界比較研究。

Leveraging TriNetX with target trial emulation and propensity-score matching to compare treatment effectiveness and assess rare events. Representative work: abemaciclib vs. palbociclib comparison in The Breast.

TriNetXPSMTTE
iii.

系統性回顧與薈萃分析 Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses

作為 Cochrane Library 的 Review Author，發表三篇 Cochrane Database 系統性回顧（CLL 維持治療、SMM 早期介入、FLT3-AML 移植後 TKI 維持），並廣泛應用網絡薈萃分析與 trial sequential analysis。

As a Cochrane Review Author, published three Cochrane Database reviews (CLL maintenance, SMM early intervention, post-transplant TKI in FLT3-AML), with broad use of network meta-analysis and trial sequential analysis.
